FAQ

Questions we hear a lot

How much does an app cost?

It depends on scope. A focused single-platform MVP usually starts in the low five figures (USD), while a two-platform app with backend, accounts and payments is more. After a discovery call you get a fixed quote with milestones, not an open-ended hourly bill.

How long does it take to launch?

Most apps go from kickoff to store release in 8 to 16 weeks. Simple utilities can be faster; apps with complex backends take longer. The timeline is set in the scope document before we start.

Who owns the code and the store accounts?

You do. We publish under your own Apple Developer and Google Play accounts, and the full source code is in a repository you control from the first commit.

Native or cross-platform, which should I pick?

Native (Swift and Kotlin) gives the best performance, platform integration and long-term maintainability. Cross-platform (Flutter or React Native) is a good fit when you need both stores quickly with a shared design and a modest budget. We recommend honestly based on your app, not on what is easiest for us.

Can you take over or port an existing app?

Yes. We regularly port iOS apps to Android with full feature parity, modernize legacy codebases and take over maintenance of apps built by other teams. We start with a short code audit so you know exactly what you are getting into.

What happens after launch?

Every project includes a post-launch support window. After that you can choose a monthly maintenance plan covering OS updates, crash fixes, store compliance and small improvements, or come back for feature releases as needed.
