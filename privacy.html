Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026. This policy applies to the Zimalify website and to every mobile app published by Zimalify on the Apple App Store and Google Play.

1. Who we are

Zimalify ("we", "us") is a mobile app studio. You can reach us at contact@zimalify.com. We are the data controller for the personal data described in this policy.

2. Data we collect

Data you give us

  • Contact form: name, email address and your message, so we can reply to you.
  • Support requests: the content of your email and any attachments you choose to send.

Data collected automatically in our apps

  • Diagnostics: crash reports and performance data (device model, OS version, app version) used only to fix bugs.
  • Usage analytics: anonymous, aggregated events such as which screens are opened, used to improve the apps. No account is required and we do not collect your name or email through the apps.
  • Advertising identifiers: only in apps that show ads, and only with your consent where the law requires it (for example App Tracking Transparency on iOS).

Data that stays on your device

Our utility apps (for example photo cleaners, scanners, trackers) process your photos, files and entries on your device. That content is not uploaded to our servers unless a feature explicitly says so and you opt in.

3. How we use data

  • To provide, maintain and improve our apps and website.
  • To respond to your messages and support requests.
  • To detect and fix crashes and security issues.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal data.

4. Third-party services

Depending on the app, we may use the following providers, each governed by their own privacy policy:

  • Apple App Store and Google Play (distribution, purchases, subscriptions)
  • Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Analytics (diagnostics and aggregated analytics)
  • Formspree (website contact form delivery)
  • Google AdMob (ads, only in free ad-supported apps)

5. Purchases and subscriptions

In-app purchases are processed by Apple or Google. We never see your payment card details. Refunds are handled through the respective store.

6. Data retention

Contact and support emails are kept for as long as needed to help you and for up to 24 months afterwards. Crash and analytics data is retained by our providers according to their retention settings, typically 90 days to 14 months.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (including the EU/EEA, UK and California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal data, and to object to or restrict its processing. Email contact@zimalify.com and we will respond within 30 days.

8. Children

Our apps are not directed at children under 13 (or the applicable age in your country) and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

We use industry-standard measures, including encryption in transit, to protect data. No method of transmission is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows the latest version. Material changes will be announced inside the affected app or on this page.

11. Contact

Questions about privacy? Email contact@zimalify.com.
