Common questions

How do I restore a purchase?

Open the app, go to Settings and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account you bought with.

How do I cancel a subscription?

iPhone/iPad: Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Android: Google Play → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Deleting an app does not cancel its subscription.

How do I request a refund?

Refunds are handled by the stores. Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com. Google: Google Play refund help. If you get stuck, email us and we will help.

How do I report a bug?

Email contact@zimalify.com with the app name, your device model, OS version and the steps that cause the problem. A screenshot or screen recording helps a lot.

How do I delete my data?

Our apps store your data on your device. Deleting the app removes it. For any data you sent us by email, request deletion at contact@zimalify.com.
